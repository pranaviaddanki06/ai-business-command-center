# PULSE — Decision Intelligence

PULSE is a polished, recruiter-facing business intelligence experience designed to turn business data into signals, explanations, forecasts, and decisions.

## Product story

**See the signal. Understand the shift. Move with confidence.**

The experience is built around a fictional retail workspace, NexaRetail, and demonstrates how an analyst can move beyond static reporting into decision-oriented analytics.

## Modules

- Executive Overview — KPI health, trajectory, regional and category signals
- Performance Intelligence — growth, channel mix and revenue/margin mapping
- Root Cause Explorer — contribution analysis and driver tree
- Decision Center — prioritized recommendations with estimated upside and confidence
- Revenue Forecast — forward-looking trajectory and risk-aware commentary
- Scenario Lab — interactive discount, inventory and marketing simulations

## Highlights

- Premium dark/light theme with a lightbulb control
- Product-style splash screen and branded PULSE identity
- Responsive sidebar navigation
- Interactive Chart.js visualizations
- Interactive scenario simulator
- Executive brief export
- Search command modal
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Synthetic business data designed for demonstration and portfolio use

## Stack

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Chart.js, Lucide Icons, Vercel.

## Portfolio note

The business data is synthetic. The product is intentionally designed to demonstrate analytical thinking, business storytelling, dashboard design and decision intelligence rather than expose confidential company data.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser or serve the folder with any static web server.

## Positioning

PULSE is designed as a flagship portfolio project for Data Analytics, Business Analytics, Business Intelligence and AI/ML-adjacent roles.
